A Bit About Me

Hello, I'm Siddharth!

I'm a product designer based in Bengaluru, India and I am constantly looking for ground-breaking solutions to issues that arise in daily life. In my work, I spend most of my time coming up with innovative solutions to user problems. (That's not entirely true, I just enjoy creating fun experiences on digital platforms but let's keep it professional 🙂).

I stumbled into NIFT Mumbai by accident, and it turned into the thing I love most. Maybe the best things arrive without a plan. What still feels like magic is watching an idea evolve into something real, something people use, rely on, and enjoy.

These days, I design for fintech, with a growing focus on scalable design systems and products that balance business goals with genuinely good user experiences.

I'm always open to meaningful conversations, full-time opportunities, and freelance projects where there's a problem worth solving.
