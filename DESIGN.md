## 8. Do's and don'ts

Design boundaries and anti-patterns. The "don't" list matters at least as much as the "do" list.

### Do

- Use light text (WCAG AA compliant) on dark backgrounds
- Use subtle borders or color shifts for depth instead of shadows
- Use the 4px base unit spacing scale consistently
- Use backdrop blur sparingly for overlay surfaces
- Keep transitions under 200ms for interactive elements
- Maintain consistent button sizing and border-radius across the app

### Don't

- Don't use dark text on dark backgrounds
- Don't use pure white (#fff) text on pure black (#000) — causes eye strain
- Don't exceed 3 font weights — currently using 4
- Don't use glass effects on small elements
- Don't mix warm and cool grays in the same surface
- Don't use more than 2 accent colors
- Don't mix different button border radii in the same interface
